
Every tweak DiaTweaks adds to Dia. Turn them on and off in **Dia → Settings → Patches**, or with the [`diatweaks` command](https://github.com/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/blob/main/README.md#the-diatweaks-command).

## Adding a tweak

Start a page titled as the tweak is in Settings, paste in [the page template](https://github.com/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/blob/main/docs/tweak-page.md), and link it from the sidebar and a row above. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#documenting-a-patch).
