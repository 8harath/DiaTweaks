Stops the downloads list opening over the page each time a download starts. The downloads button still shows the download, and clicking it opens the list.

## Why use it

Dia pops the downloads list over the page whenever a download starts, which covers what you're reading.

- Handy if you download a lot, or start downloads from pages you want to keep looking at.
- You still see progress on the button and can open the list when you want.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Don’t Open Downloads List**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable quietDownloads
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with tabs at the top?</strong></summary>

Yes. The list stays closed there too, and the downloads button at the right of the tab bar opens it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with VoiceOver?</strong></summary>

Yes. Pressing the downloads button with VoiceOver or Full Keyboard Access opens the list as usual.

</details>
