
Hides the recent tabs button: the small arrow next to **Chats** at the bottom of the sidebar, or its button in the tab bar when tabs are at the top.

| Tabs in the sidebar | Tabs at the top |
|:---:|:---:|
| ![Hide recent tabs button off, then on, with tabs in the sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideRecentTabsButton.webp) | ![Hide recent tabs button off, then on, with tabs at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/horizontal-hideRecentTabsButton.webp) |

## Why use it

If you never use the recent tabs list, its button is clutter next to Chats.

- Handy if you want a quieter bottom of the sidebar or tab bar.
- Skip it if you reopen recent tabs from there.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Recent Tabs Button**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideRecentTabsButton
```
