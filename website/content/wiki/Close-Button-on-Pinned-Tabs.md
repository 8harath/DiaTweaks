Shows a close button when you point at a pinned tab outside a folder, like Dia already does for tabs in a pinned folder.

## Why use it

Dia hides the close button on pinned tabs that aren't in a folder. This shows it when you point at the tab.

- Handy if you pin tabs and want to close them with a click.
- Skip it if you like pinned tabs that can't be closed by accident.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Close Button on Pinned Tabs**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable pinnedTabCloseButton
```

It's off by default. It works with tabs in the sidebar and at the top.

## Works with

[Minus Button on Open Pinned Tabs](Minus-Button-on-Open-Pinned-Tabs) swaps the ✕ for a – on pinned tabs with a page open, so you can close the page and keep the tab.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What does the button do?</strong></summary>

It closes the pinned tab and removes it, like the button on tabs in a pinned folder.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it change pinned tabs in a folder?</strong></summary>

No. Dia already shows the button there.
