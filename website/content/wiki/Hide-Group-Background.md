Leaves an open tab group without the grey box Dia draws behind it, so groups look like Arc's folders. A group you've given a color keeps it.

![Hide Group Background off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideTabGroupBackground.webp)

## Why use it

The grey box behind a tab group adds visual weight, especially with several groups open.

- Handy if you want groups to look like Arc's folders.
- Colored groups keep their color, so you still tell them apart.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Group Background**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideTabGroupBackground
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it change groups I picked a color for?</strong></summary>

No. Only the grey a group gets when it has no color of its own goes.

</details>
