Darkens the page behind the [command bar](Open-New-Tabs-in-Command-Bar) while it's open.

![Darken Page Behind off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-commandBarDarken.webp)

## Why use it

Dimming the page makes the command bar stand out, especially over a bright page.
Turn it off if you want the page to keep its colors, or set **Darkness** low for a light dim.

## Turn it off

It's on by default. Open **Dia → Settings → Patches → Open New Tabs in Command Bar** and, under **Darken**, switch off **Darken Page Behind**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks disable commandBarDarken
```

## Options

Under **Darken** on the [command bar](Open-New-Tabs-in-Command-Bar) page, **Darkness** sets how dark the page gets (24% to begin with).

## Needs

[Command bar](Open-New-Tabs-in-Command-Bar). Settings greys out this switch until that's on, and `diatweaks enable commandBarDarken` offers to turn it on too.

## Works with

[Blur Page Behind](Blur-Page-Behind). With both on, the page is blurred and darkened.
