Stops the card with a pinned tab's title, address and buttons from popping up when you point at the tab. Click the tab as usual; right-click it for the same actions.

![Hide Pinned Tab Card off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hidePinnedTabCard.webp)

## Why use it

The card pops up whenever you point at a pinned tab, even when you're just passing over it.

- Handy if you move through pinned tabs often and find the card distracting.
- Skip it if you use the card's buttons often.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Pinned Tab Card**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hidePinnedTabCard
```
