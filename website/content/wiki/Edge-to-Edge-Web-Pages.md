
Dia leaves a thin gap between the page and the edges of the window. This tweak removes it, so the page reaches the edges.

## Why use it

Dia pads the page with a few pixels of window color on every side. That strip wastes room and makes the page look like a floating card.

- If you want more of the screen for the page, or a cleaner look, switch it on.
- Skip it if you like the framed look Dia ships with.

## Turn it on

It's on by default. Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Edge-to-Edge Web Pages**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable edgeToEdge
```

## Options

On the same page, once it's on:

- [Square Corners Next to Sidebar](Square-Corners-Next-to-Sidebar) gives the page straight edges where it meets the sidebar. It's on by default.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Why is the gap back when Chat is open?</strong></summary>

With Chat open, Dia shows the page as a card next to it. Edge-to-edge steps aside until you close Chat.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What happens in full screen?</strong></summary>

The page reaches the edges of the screen, and its corners there are square.

</details>
