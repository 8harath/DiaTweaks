Gives each profile an emoji, which takes the place of its dot in the profile switcher.

## Why use it

Profiles are told apart only by small colored dots, which look alike when you have several.

- Handy if you switch between work, personal and other profiles often.
- An emoji is faster to spot than a dot color.

## Turn it on

It's on by default. To check, open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and look for **Profile Emoji**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable profileEmoji
```

Then open **Dia → Settings → Profiles**, choose a profile, and click the **Emoji** field under Theme Color to pick one. Clear the field to get the dot back.
