
Hides the "Looking for chat? Upgrade to Better Days." banner that Dia shows under the command bar as you type, if your account has no chat usage left.

![Hide upgrade banner off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideUpgradeBanner.webp)

## Why use it

If your account has no chat usage, the command bar keeps showing an upgrade offer as you type. This hides it so only your suggestions are left.
It does nothing if you have chat usage.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Upgrade Banner**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideUpgradeBanner
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Why don't I see a difference?</strong></summary>

Dia only shows the banner to accounts without chat usage. With chat usage there's nothing to hide.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What about Command bar?</strong></summary>

[Command bar](Open-New-Tabs-in-Command-Bar) doesn't show the banner either way: it sits under the new tab page, which Command bar hides.

</details>
