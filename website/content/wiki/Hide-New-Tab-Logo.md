Hides the Dia logo above the search field on new tab pages.

![Hide New Tab Logo off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideNewTabLogo.webp)

## Why use it

If you want a plainer new tab page, this removes the logo and leaves the search field.
It only hides the logo. The search field stays where it was, so there's an empty space above it.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide New Tab Logo**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideNewTabLogo
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Does the search field move?</strong></summary>

No. It stays where it was, with the space above it left empty.

</details>
