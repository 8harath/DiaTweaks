Closed a tab by mistake? Press ⌘Z straight away and it comes back, like History › Reopen Closed Tab (⇧⌘T). You can set another shortcut on its Settings page. It only works right after closing: once you type or click, ⌘Z goes back to undoing whatever you're doing.

## Why use it

It's easy to close the wrong tab, and ⇧⌘T is a long reach. This lets ⌘Z, which you already press to undo, bring the tab back.
Handy if you undo by habit. It only takes the shortcut right after a tab closes, so it doesn't get in the way of undo in text fields. If you'd rather keep ⇧⌘T, leave it off.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Undo Closing a Tab**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable undoCloseTab
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it get in the way of ⌘Z in text fields?</strong></summary>

No. ⌘Z only reopens a tab if closing it was the last thing you did. Typing, clicking or any other key in between hands ⌘Z back to the page or field you're in.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What about undoing Tidy Tabs?</strong></summary>

⌘Z undoes whichever happened last. Tidy your tabs and then close one, and ⌘Z reopens the tab; tidy after closing, and ⌘Z undoes the tidy.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with tabs at the top?</strong></summary>

Yes, in both layouts.

</details>
