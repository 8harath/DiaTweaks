With [Peek on Shift-Click](Peek-on-Shift-Click) on, a link that leaves a pinned tab's site opens in Peek, like Arc, instead of in a new tab. Links on the same site still open in the pinned tab.

![Peek from Pinned Tabs, off and on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-peekPinnedLinks.png)

## Why use it

A pinned tab is meant to stay on one site. Without this, a link that leaves the site opens a new tab and adds clutter next to your pins.

Use it for pinned mail, chat or feed tabs where you often follow links out. Turn it off if you want those links as normal tabs.

## Turn it on

It's on by default once Peek on Shift-Click is on. To change it, open **Dia → Settings → Patches → Peek on Shift-Click** and, under **Options**, use the **Peek from Pinned Tabs** switch. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable peekPinnedLinks
```

## Needs

[Peek on Shift-Click](Peek-on-Shift-Click). Settings greys out this switch until that's on, and `diatweaks enable peekPinnedLinks` offers to turn it on too.
