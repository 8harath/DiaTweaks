
Press **Escape** to close the [Little Dia](Little-Dia) window and go back to the app the link came from.

## Why use it

A quick look at a link should take one key to dismiss, the way it does in Little Arc. Turn it off if you use sites where Escape matters, like a dialog or a game, since with this on the page never sees Escape.

## Turn it on

It's on by default once Little Dia is on. To change it, open **Dia → Settings → Patches → Little Dia** and, under **Options**, use the **Close with Escape** switch. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable littleDiaEscapeCloses
```

## Needs

[Little Dia](Little-Dia). Settings greys out this switch until that's on, and `diatweaks enable littleDiaEscapeCloses` offers to turn it on too.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What if I'm typing an address?</strong></summary>

Escape cancels the typing first. Press it again to close the window. In full screen, Escape is left to the page.
