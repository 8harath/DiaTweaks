Press a keyboard shortcut to hide or show the toolbar, as [Hide Toolbar](Hide-Toolbar) does. It's ⌃D unless you set another.

## Why use it

A shortcut is the quickest way to flip between a clean page and the full toolbar, and it doesn't depend on where the pointer is.

- Use it if you like to hide the toolbar for reading and bring it back for a moment.
- It works even when Hide Toolbar is off, so one key switches the whole feature.
- Change the shortcut if ⌃D clashes with forward delete in text fields.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches → Hide Toolbar** and, under **Showing the Toolbar**, switch on **Toggle toolbar visibility**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable toggleToolbarVisibility
```

## Change the shortcut

Under the switch, click **Shortcut** and press the keys you want. It needs ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, unless it's a function key. Press Delete to clear it, or Escape to keep the one you had.

## Needs

[Hide Toolbar](Hide-Toolbar). Settings lists this switch on that tweak's page. Once it's on, the shortcut works whether the toolbar is hidden or showing.
