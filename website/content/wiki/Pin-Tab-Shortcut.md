
Press **⌘D** to move the tab you're on to the bottom of your pinned tabs, like in Arc. Press it on a pinned tab to unpin it.

## Why use it

Dia's own Pin/Unpin Tab shortcut adds the tab to your favorites at the top. Putting a tab with your pinned tabs, above the line, is only in a tab's right-click menu as **Pin as Tab**. This puts it on a key.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Pin Tab Shortcut**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable pinTabShortcut
```

## Change the shortcut

It's **⌘D** to begin with. Under the switch, click **Shortcut** and press the keys you want. It needs ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, unless it's a function key. Press Delete to clear it, or Escape to keep the one you had.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What happened to Bookmark This Page?</strong></summary>

With this on, ⌘D pins instead. It still bookmarks when there's nothing to pin: on a favorite, on a tab inside a pinned folder, or in a window without tabs. Set another shortcut to keep ⌘D for bookmarks.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where does an unpinned tab go?</strong></summary>

Where Dia puts new tabs, the same as choosing **Unpin** from its right-click menu.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>I pinned a tab and it disappeared.</strong></summary>

With [Collapse Pinned Tabs](Collapse-Pinned-Tabs) folded, a tab you pin joins the folded pinned tabs. Unfold them to see it.

</details>
