Removes the colored border around the selected pinned tab. The tab keeps its tint, so you can still tell which one is selected.

| Tabs in the sidebar | Tabs at the top |
|:---:|:---:|
| ![Hide pinned tab border off, then on, with tabs in the sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hidePinnedTabBorder.webp) | ![Hide pinned tab border off, then on, with tabs at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/horizontal-hidePinnedTabBorder.webp) |

## Why use it

The colored border on the selected pinned tab can look heavy next to the tint.

- Handy if you want a quieter, flatter look.
- Skip it if you rely on the border to spot the selected tab.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Pinned Tab Border**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hidePinnedTabBorder
```
