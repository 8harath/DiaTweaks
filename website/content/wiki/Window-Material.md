Puts a macOS material or Liquid Glass behind the tabs and sidebar, still tinted with your profile's color, so they pick up what is behind the window.

![Every material and Liquid Glass in light mode, one second each](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-windowMaterial.webp)

## Why use it

Dia's window is a flat color. If you like the translucent look other Mac apps have, this gives Dia the same, and you can pick from every material and Liquid Glass style macOS has.

Handy when you want Dia to match macOS 26 or a wallpaper you like. Skip it if you want text on the sidebar to stay as high contrast as possible, since translucent materials let busy backgrounds show through.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Window Material**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable windowMaterial
```

Pick the material on the same page, under **Options → Material**. It's Abutted Sidebar glass to begin with.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What can I pick?</strong></summary>

Every material macOS offers apps, such as Sidebar, Popover and HUD Window, and on macOS 26 every kind of Liquid Glass macOS has, including ones it keeps for itself, like Dock, Control Center and Abutted Sidebar. A few, like Sidebar, are nearly solid in light mode. Every one is pictured above.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why does the new tab page look like glass too?</strong></summary>

It has no background of its own, so it shows the window's. Web pages draw their own.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I pick the material from Terminal?</strong></summary>

Not yet. `diatweaks` turns it on and off, and Settings picks the material.

</details>
