Press a shortcut for a menu of your extensions at the top right of the page. Choosing one opens it as clicking its toolbar button would, pinned or not.

![Extensions Shortcut](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-extensionsShortcut.webp)

## Why use it

Dia only reaches an extension through its toolbar button, and unpinned ones are buried. This lists them all from one key.

- Handy if you use extensions often, or hide the toolbar.
- Useful if you rely on the keyboard more than the mouse.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Extensions Shortcut**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable extensionsShortcut
```

## Change the shortcut

It's **⇧⌘X** to begin with. Under the switch, click **Shortcut** and press the keys you want. It needs ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, unless it's a function key. Press Delete to clear it, or Escape to keep the one you had.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Which extensions does it list?</strong></summary>

Every extension that's on and has a toolbar button, whether or not it's pinned.

</details>
