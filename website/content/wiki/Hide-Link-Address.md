Hides the address that shows at the bottom of the page when you point at a link.

![Hide Link Address off, then on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideLinkAddress.webp)

## Why use it

Dia shows a link's address in a bar at the bottom of the page when you point at it, which can cover content and distract.

Use it for a cleaner page. You lose the quick check of where a link goes, so skip it if you use that to spot suspicious links.

## Turn it on

It's off by default. Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Link Address**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideLinkAddress
```

## Works with

[Peek on Shift-Click](Peek-on-Shift-Click). Holding Shift over a link still starts loading it with the address hidden.
