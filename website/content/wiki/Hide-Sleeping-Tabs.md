Leaves tabs with nothing loaded, like a pinned tab you closed, out of the tab switcher (**⌃⇥**), instead of showing them as empty pictures. The switcher fits the tabs left, and cycling skips the others.

## Why use it

The tab switcher shows a tab with nothing loaded, like a pinned tab you closed, as an empty picture, and cycling lands on it. This leaves those tabs out so every stop has a page to show.
Handy if you pin many tabs. You can still reach a hidden tab from the sidebar.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Sleeping Tabs**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideSleepingTabs
```
