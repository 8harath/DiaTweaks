
Hides the **Chats** button at the bottom of the sidebar, or in the tab bar when tabs are at the top.

| Tabs in the sidebar | Tabs at the top |
|:---:|:---:|
| ![Hide Chats button off, then on, with tabs in the sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/vertical-hideChatsButton.webp) | ![Hide Chats button off, then on, with tabs at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/JaceThings/DiaTweaks/previews/horizontal-hideChatsButton.webp) |

## Why use it

If you don't use Chat history, the Chats button is clutter in the sidebar or tab bar.

- Handy if you want a quieter bottom of the sidebar.
- Skip it if you open past chats from there.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Chats Button**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideChatsButton
```
