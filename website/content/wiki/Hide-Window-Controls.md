Hides the close, minimize and zoom buttons. ⌘W, ⌘M and the Window menu still work, and you can still drag the window by the sidebar or tab bar.

## Why use it

The close, minimize and zoom buttons take up space at the top of the sidebar or tab bar, and most people close windows with ⌘W anyway.

Use it for a cleaner, tighter top edge. You can't close a window by clicking, so skip it if you do that often.

## Turn it on

Open **Dia → Settings → Patches** and switch on **Hide Window Controls**. Or run:

```sh
diatweaks enable hideTrafficLights
```

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Why does the sidebar show my profile's name?</strong></summary>

That's what Dia shows in the space the buttons leave. Clicking it switches profiles as before.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with tabs at the top?</strong></summary>

Yes. The pinned tabs move into the space.

</details>
